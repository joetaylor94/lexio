Privacy policy

Last updated 18 November 2026

The short version

  • You don’t need to give your name or email to use Lexio News.
  • We store your learning progress so the app works, and nothing more.
  • There are no ads and no tracking across other apps or websites.
  • You can delete everything from inside the app at any time.

Who we are

Lexio News (“the app”) is run by Joseph D Taylor Ltd (“we”, “us”). We are the controller of the personal data described here. You can reach us at support@lexiolabs.io.

What we collect

DataWhy we need it
An anonymous account ID, created automatically when you first open the appTo keep your progress together and separate from everyone else’s
Display name, only if you choose to type oneTo show it on your profile
Learning data: the languages you study, your reading level, the words you have marked or learned, articles finished, quiz and practice results, XP, streak and the days you readTo choose stories at your level, build your word list and show your progress
Settings: daily goal, reminder on or off and its time, sound and text sizeTo remember your preferences
Purchase information: which plan you bought, when it renews or expires, and an App Store transaction IDTo unlock the app and restore your purchase
Technical data: IP address, device type and app version, as sent with any internet requestTo deliver the service and keep it secure. We don’t use it to identify you

We do not collect your location, contacts, photos, microphone audio or advertising identifier. We never see your card details: payments are handled entirely by Apple.

Notifications

If you turn on the morning reminder, the app schedules it on your phone. No notification token is sent to us, and you can turn reminders off in the app or in iOS Settings.

How we use your data

Only to run the app: showing stories at your level, tracking your vocabulary and progress, unlocking what you paid for, and answering you if you write to us. We don’t use your data for advertising or sell it, and we don’t use it to train AI models.

Under UK and EU data protection law, our legal bases are performance of a contract (providing the app you subscribed to) and our legitimate interests in keeping the service secure and working.

Who we share it with

We use a small number of service providers, who process data on our behalf:

ProviderWhat they handle
SupabaseHosts our database: your anonymous ID, display name, learning data and settings
RevenueCatManages subscriptions: your anonymous ID and purchase information
AppleProcesses payments and delivers the app through the App Store
OpenAITurns article text into speech when you tap the speaker. Only the word or sentence from the article is sent; nothing about you goes with it

We may also disclose data if the law requires it. If the app is ever sold or transferred, your data would move with it under the same protections.

International transfers

Some of these providers store or process data outside the UK and the European Economic Area, including in the United States. Where they do, the transfer is covered by safeguards recognised in law, such as standard contractual clauses.

How long we keep it

We keep your data for as long as you use the app. When you delete your account, your profile and learning data are erased from our database straight away. Apple and RevenueCat keep records of purchases for as long as tax and accounting rules require.

Deleting your data

You can delete your data in the Me tab of the app. This permanently deletes your account, words and progress from your phone and from our servers. It cannot be undone, and it does not cancel a subscription: do that in your App Store settings.

If you no longer have the app, write to support@lexiolabs.io and we’ll help. Because accounts are anonymous, we may need details from you to find the right one.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you have the right to see the data we hold about you, have it corrected or deleted, receive a copy, and object to or restrict how we use it. To use any of these, write to support@lexiolabs.io. You also have the right to complain to your data protection authority; in the UK that is the Information Commissioner’s Office (ico.org.uk).

Children

Lexio News is not aimed at children under 13, and we don’t knowingly collect data from them. The stories cover real news, including conflict, crime and health. If you believe a child has used the app, contact us and we’ll delete the data.

Security

Data travels over encrypted connections, and each account can read only its own records. No system is perfectly secure, but we keep what we hold to a minimum so there is little to lose.

Changes to this policy

If we change what we collect or how we use it, we’ll update this page and the date at the top. For significant changes we’ll also tell you in the app.
