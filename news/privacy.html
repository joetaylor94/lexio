Privacy policy

Last updated 26 November 2026

The short version

  • You sign up with Apple or an email address. There is no password.
  • We store your account details and your learning progress so the app works, and nothing more.
  • We may email you reminders and news. You can switch that off at any time.
  • There are no ads and no tracking across other apps or websites.
  • You can delete everything from inside the app at any time.

Who we are

Lexio News (“the app”) is run by Joseph D Taylor Ltd (“we”, “us”). We are the controller of the personal data described here. You can reach us at support@lexiolabs.io.

We do not collect your location, contacts, photos, microphone audio or advertising identifier. We never see your card details: payments are handled entirely by Apple.

Emails

We send two kinds of email. Account emails, such as the code you sign in with, are needed to run your account and are always sent. Reminders and news, such as a nudge to keep reading or word of a new feature, are optional: you choose when you sign up, and you can switch them off at any time under Emails in the Me tab or by writing to us. We don’t share your email address with advertisers or anyone else for their own use.

Notifications

If you turn on the morning reminder, the app schedules it on your phone. No notification token is sent to us, and you can turn reminders off in the app or in iOS Settings.

How we use your data

To run the app: signing you in, showing stories at your level, tracking your vocabulary and progress, unlocking what you paid for, and answering you if you write to us. And, unless you have switched them off, to send you reminders and news about Lexio by email. We don’t use your data for advertising other people’s products or sell it, and we don’t use it to train AI models.

Under UK and EU data protection law, our legal bases are performance of a contract (providing the account and the app you subscribed to) and our legitimate interests in keeping the service secure and working and in telling our own customers about our own service. Where the law asks for your consent to reminders and news by email, we rely on the choice you make when you sign up, which you can withdraw at any time.

International transfers

Some of these providers store or process data outside the UK and the European Economic Area, including in the United States. Where they do, the transfer is covered by safeguards recognised in law, such as standard contractual clauses.

How long we keep it

We keep your data for as long as you use the app. When you delete your account, your email address, your link to Apple, your profile and your learning data are erased from our database straight away. Emails we have already sent stay in our email provider’s records for a short time, and emails you sent to us stay in our inbox until we no longer need them. Apple and RevenueCat keep records of purchases for as long as tax and accounting rules require.

Deleting your data

You can delete your data in the Me tab of the app. This permanently deletes your account, email address, words and progress from your phone and from our servers. If you signed up with Apple, Apple asks you to confirm it is you, and we then ask Apple to stop sharing your Apple ID with the app. Deleting cannot be undone, and it does not cancel a subscription: do that in your App Store settings.

Signing out is different: it only removes the account from that phone, and everything stays for when you sign in again.

If you no longer have the app, write to support@lexiolabs.io from the email address on your account and we’ll delete it for you. If you signed up with Apple and hid your email address, we may need a few details from you to find the right account.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you have the right to see the data we hold about you, have it corrected or deleted, receive a copy, and object to or restrict how we use it. To use any of these, write to support@lexiolabs.io. You also have the right to complain to your data protection authority; in the UK that is the Information Commissioner’s Office (ico.org.uk).

Children

Lexio News is not aimed at children under 13, and we don’t knowingly collect data from them. The stories cover real news, including conflict, crime and health. If you believe a child has used the app, contact us and we’ll delete the data.

Security

Data travels over encrypted connections, there are no passwords to steal, and each account can read only its own records. No system is perfectly secure, but we keep what we hold to a minimum so there is little to lose.

Changes to this policy

If we change what we collect or how we use it, we’ll update this page and the date at the top. For significant changes we’ll also tell you in the app.
