Support

Answers to common questions. If yours isn’t here, write to us.

Contact us

Email support@lexiolabs.io. We aim to reply within two working days. It helps if you tell us your iPhone model and what you were doing when the problem happened.

Questions

How do I cancel my subscription?

Open the Settings app on your iPhone, tap your name, then Subscriptions, and choose Lexio News. In the app, Me → Subscription → Manage takes you to the same place. You keep access until the end of the period you paid for.

How do I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple. Go to reportaproblem.apple.com, sign in, and choose “Request a refund” for your Lexio News purchase.

I paid, but the app is asking me to subscribe again

Tap “Restore purchases” at the bottom of the subscription screen. Make sure you’re signed in to the same Apple account you bought with.

How do I change my reading level?

Go to Me → Reading level. “Auto” follows the number of words you know. You can also fix it to a level; the app switches back to Auto once your vocabulary catches up.

How does the app decide my level?

By the number of words you know. Each level begins at a set number, from A1 at the start to C2 at 16,000 words. Words become “known” when you finish a story or mark them yourself.

I’m not getting my morning reminder

Check that Morning reminder is on in the Me tab, and that notifications are allowed for Lexio News in iOS Settings. You won’t get one on a day you’ve already read.

There’s no sound

Check Sound effects is on in the Me tab and that your volume is up. Sounds and speech play even when your phone is on silent. The first time a word is spoken it can take a second or two.

My sign-in code hasn’t arrived

Codes come from hello@lexiolabs.io and usually arrive within a minute. Check your junk folder, and check the address on the code screen is spelled correctly. You can ask for a new code from the same screen; only the newest one works, and each lasts an hour.

I have a new phone. How do I get my words back?

On the first screen, tap “I already have an account” and sign in the same way you signed up: with Apple, or with the same email address. Your words and progress come back with your account. Then tap “Restore purchases” if the app asks you to subscribe.

How do I delete my account and data?

Go to the Me tab and tap Delete next to “Delete account”. This removes your account, email address, words and progress for good. It doesn’t cancel a subscription, so do that first in your App Store settings. If you no longer have the app, email us from the address on your account and we’ll delete it for you.

I found a mistake in a story or a translation

Thank you. Email us the story’s headline and what’s wrong, and we’ll fix it. Stories are written with the help of AI and we want to hear about errors.
